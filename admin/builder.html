NextPath Course Builder

Turns your master course sheet into data.js and match.js. Runs only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Step 1. In Google Sheets: File > Download > Comma-separated values (.csv). Choose that file here.
Step 2. Set the report date and version. Raise the version each time (1.9, 2.0, 2.1).
Step 3.
Choose your current data.js and match.js (for example after an AI tool edited them). This checks them for mistakes before you upload to GitHub.

Keep the master sheet private in Google Drive. This page and the files you publish are public, so never put commissions, internal notes or student data in the sheet.
